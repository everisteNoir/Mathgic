## Δ · The Chapter of Polyhedra

The five Platonic solids of Plato's *Timaeus*, each bound to an element and awake with its own living core.

| Solid | Element | Core |
|---|---|---|
| Tetrahedron | **Fire** · *Pyr* | A flickering flame that always points upward, and rising embers |
| Hexahedron (Cube) | **Earth** · *Gē* | A levitating emerald crystal and a runic stone ring |
| Octahedron | **Air** · *Aēr* | A helical wind vortex |
| Icosahedron | **Water** · *Hydōr* | An undulating liquid orb |
| Dodecahedron | **Aether** · *Kosmos* | A celestial astrolabe cage around a starlight core |

Every solid rests on a flat **base face**. Choose one from the dock at the bottom and the new solid materializes from nothing; sections that hide the solid dissolve it smoothly.

**Net unfolding.** Drag *2D Geometric Net Unfolding* and the solid levels itself onto its base, the base stays perfectly still, and the other faces swing open flat. The camera climbs to look down on the net. At 100% the elemental core departs in its own way:

- **Fire** — the flame stretches into a thin tongue and ascends as embers
- **Earth** — the crystal shatters into emerald shards and a seismic ring spreads
- **Air** — the vortex unwinds into streamlines that fan across the net, with swirling dust and wind rings
- **Water** — the orb falls onto the base, splashes into a crown of droplets that rain down, and ripples spread to the edges
- **Aether** — the cage expands, the star goes supernova and collapses, the halo flies away

**Press and hold.** Hold a solid and motes stream inward while three rune rings, shaped like the solid's own faces, spin and tighten. Release when fully charged for an elemental burst, a shockwave, a flash of bloom and a slight tremor of the world.

**Dual Twin.** In the spellbook, *Summon Twin* conjures the dual solid inside the crystal: a band of light writes it upward from the base while sparks flare at its vertices. *Dismiss Twin* unwrites it. The dual's vertices are the face centres of the solid: cube ⇄ octahedron, icosahedron ⇄ dodecahedron, tetrahedron ⇄ itself.

## φ · The Chapter of the Golden Ratio

A standalone chapter (no polyhedron on stage) with five patterns of φ = 1.6180339887…

- **Rectangles** — three orthogonal Golden Rectangles, with an optional gyroscope spin
- **Spiral** — Fibonacci squares and the golden spiral; *Auto-grow* builds it square by square while F(n+1)/F(n) closes in on φ
- **Seeds** — a sunflower laid out by the golden angle (≈ 137.508°). Drag the angle away from it and the even packing collapses into radial arms; light up the **8, 13, 21 or 34** Fibonacci arms
- **Pentagram** — nested pentagrams, each 1/φ² the size of the last
- **Golden Cut** — drag a division point and chase the one place where whole ÷ long = long ÷ short

## π · The Chapter of Projection

A standalone chapter with three lenses.

### Shadows
An **n-cube from 2D to 6D** cast down into 3D one dimension at a time, then onto a 2D floor shadow and a 1D line shadow. Choose parallel or **perspective** projection, x′ = x·d / (d − xₖ), and move the eye: vertices that reach d ≤ xₖ escape to infinity and fade away. Rotate in every plane at once or only XW, YW or ZW, and slide the **extrusion** to watch a square grow into a cube and onward.

### Quotient v ~ λv
The heart of projective geometry. In **ℝ² → ℝP¹** and **ℝ³ → ℝP²**, every nonzero vector is identified with all of its multiples λv.

- **Vector space** — sample vectors with the line through the origin that holds each class (one colour per class). The origin is a hollow ring: 0 belongs to no line, so it is not a point.
- **Collapse** — slide every class along its line to its single representative: onto the plane z = 1 (the **affine chart**) or onto the **sphere / ±**. Directions with z = 0 fly off to infinity.
- **Side by side** — the vector space beside P(V), with light linking v and λv to the same projective point. In the chart, ℝ² is squeezed into a disc whose gold rim is the **line at infinity**.
- **Parallels meeting at infinity** — two parallel lines of the chart are two planes through the origin; they meet in a direction of z = 0, a point at infinity.
- **Act with H** — let the 3×3 matrix of the Ladder act on V and watch whole classes move to classes.

### Geometry Ladder
Euclidean ⊂ Affine ⊂ Projective, on one plane. Rotation and shifts keep lengths and angles; stretches and shears keep only parallelism; the projective tilt keeps only straightness and the cross-ratio, and the image of the line at infinity appears as a gold **horizon**. The readout names which geometry you are in.

## ✧ The Effects Switch

Everything above moves smoothly, with one easing language across the interface: rippling buttons, glowing sliders, a light that glides between options, a portal flash between chapters, and soft chimes when sound is on.

**FX: Off** enters a calm **static mode**: no ambient particles, hold effects, ripples, portal flash or special dissolves (cores simply shrink as the net opens), and no bloom boost. The mathematics and every control stay exactly the same. The choice is remembered, and it starts **Off** automatically if your system asks for reduced motion.

## ☄ Raising the Grimoire

Mathgic is a zero-build static site (plain ES modules, Three.js from a CDN). Serve the folder with any static server, for example `python3 -m http.server`, and open it in a browser. (Opening `index.html` directly from disk will not work: browsers block ES modules from `file://`.)

**GitHub → Cloudflare Pages**

1. Upload everything (`index.html`, `README.md`, `css/`, `js/`) to a GitHub repository.
2. Connect it to **Cloudflare Pages** with *Framework preset* `None`, an empty *Build command*, and *Build output directory* `/`.
3. **Save and Deploy.**

If a new version looks like the old one, hard-refresh (Ctrl+F5). The *Build* number at the bottom of the menu tells you which version is running.

## ⚒ The Craft (for those who add spells)

```
index.html          the stage, the menu and the panels
css/style.css       the theme and the motion language
js/main.js          wires the engine, the spells and the interface
js/core/            Engine (render loop, interaction), HoldFX, AudioEngine, FXState
js/solids/          PlatonicMath (geometry, nets, resting poses), SolidMeshBuilder (solids and cores)
js/spells/          one file per chapter, plus SpellManager
js/ui/              UIController (menu, panel, share link), FantasyUI (micro-interactions)
```

To add a chapter: copy `js/spells/_TemplateSpell.js`, register it in `js/main.js`, then add its menu button and its empty panel in `index.html` (the template explains how). Controls can be sliders, `choice` groups or `toggle` switches, and any of them can use `showWhen` to appear only in certain modes.
