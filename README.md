# ❖ Mathgic — Sacred Geometry & Non-Euclidean Laboratory

An interactive 3D mathematical & elemental web laboratory exploring the **Five Platonic Solids** paired with the **Five Classical Elements from Plato's *Timaeus*** (Fire, Earth, Air, Water, and Aether), **Inscribed Sacred Geometry**, **4D Hypercubes**, and **Non-Euclidean Curvature Manifolds**.

---

## ✨ Features

1. **Sheer Crystal Frames, Luminous Gradient Edges & Volumetric Elemental Cores:**
   - **Tetrahedron (Fire • *Pyr*):** Blazing **Red–Orange–Gold** luminous edges framing an animated 3D flickering volumetric flame burning inside, with rising embers.
   - **Hexahedron / Cube (Earth • *Gē*):** **Emerald–Jade–Mint** glowing edges housing a levitating mineral octahedron and runic stone ring.
   - **Octahedron (Air • *Aēr*):** **Azure–Cyan–Ice** edges containing swirling 3D helical wind vortex ribbons responsive to cursor movement.
   - **Icosahedron (Water • *Hydōr*):** **Cobalt–Ocean–Aqua** edges surrounding an undulating harmonic liquid orb.
   - **Dodecahedron (Aether • *Kosmos*):** **Violet–Orchid–Starlight** edges enclosing a celestial astrolabe cage and golden starlight core.
2. **Zero-Overlap Mathgic Spell Modules (`js/spells/`):**
   - **Dual Polyhedron Morphing (*Inscribed*):** Summons the dual solid nested cleanly inside the sheer crystal frame.
   - **Golden Ratio X-Ray ($\phi = 1.618$, *Inscribed*):** Reveals three orthogonal Golden Rectangles and a 3D Fibonacci spiral inside the solid.
   - **4D Hypercube Portal (*Tesseract*, *Hyper-Spatial Portal*):** Automatically tucks away the central Platonic frame so the rotating 4D hypercube projects unobstructed.
   - **Non-Euclidean Space (*Curvature K*, *Hyper-Spatial Portal*):** Continuously warps space between **Hyperbolic saddle geometry ($K < 0$)**, **Flat Euclidean space ($K = 0$)**, and **Riemannian spherical curvature ($K > 0$)** with a live curved geodesic triangle.
